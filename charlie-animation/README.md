# CHARLIE — *an ordinary Tuesday*

A ~74-second, Pixar-style Blender short about one day in the life of Charlie, 34.
It's an original story about life in your 30s now: hitting snooze, getting buried in
notifications before the coffee kicks in, back-to-back video calls, group chats that
never turn into plans, and a houseplant you're quietly keeping alive. Then a small
moment where everything slows down.

Everything is generated by code: the character, sets, lighting, animation, camera cuts
and soundtrack. No external assets are needed.

## The story

| # | Scene | Time | What happens |
|---|-------|------|--------------|
| 1 | Title | | **CHARLIE**, *an ordinary Tuesday* |
| 2 | Bedroom | 6:47 AM | Alarm buzzes. Charlie hits snooze without opening their eyes and drifts back off with a smile. The phone buzzes again: **7:12**. Eyes snap open. |
| 3 | Kitchen | 7:58 AM | Phone in one hand, coffee in the other. Notifications stack up faster and faster: Slack, 47 unread emails, stand-up, rent due, the group chat, *"Mom: call me when u can"*. Charlie lets out a big sigh, then takes a sip of coffee. |
| 4 | Desk | 9:02 AM → 6:38 PM | Working from home on a call grid of mostly "camera off" initials. As the day passes in time-lapse, emails pile up, empty mugs multiply, the sun moves across the room, the desk plant wilts and a sticky note on the wall says **CALL MOM BACK!!** Charlie ends the day with a stretch and a yawn. |
| 5 | Couch | 7:41 PM | Takeout, TV glow, scrolling. The group chat goes *"we NEED to catch up!!"* … *"next month then!"* Then **Battery 1%**, and the screen goes dark. In the quiet, Charlie notices the drooping plant. |
| 6 | Window | 7:52 PM | Charlie waters the plant with the last of the coffee mug. It perks up and a flower blooms. Charlie raises the blinds and golden sunset light floods in. |
| 7 | Outside | | Leaning on the sill, Charlie sees the neighbour across the way wave and waves back. The camera pulls back as the windows in the building light up one by one, each one somebody else's ordinary Tuesday. |
| 8 | End card | | *Charlie, 34.* · Inbox: 99+ · Plans with friends: next month (probably) · Plant: alive · Charlie: doing okay. |

## Files

| File | What it is |
|------|------------|
| `build_charlie.py` | Builds the whole film in Blender (Blender 4.2+ / 5.x, or the `bpy` pip module). |
| `make_soundtrack.py` | Synthesises the score and sound effects (numpy only), synced to the frame timeline. |
| `charlie.blend` | The generated Blender file. Open it and press play, or render it. |
| `charlie.mp4` | The rendered film (960×540, 24 fps, with sound). |
| `render.sh` | One-shot pipeline: build → render frames → soundtrack → mp4. |

## Rebuild / render

```bash
# open in Blender to explore / tweak
blender charlie.blend

# or rebuild everything from the script
blender -b -P build_charlie.py -- --save charlie.blend

# render (Cycles). Defaults to 960x540 @ 14 samples + denoise; go higher if you have a GPU
blender -b -P build_charlie.py -- --render frames/ --res 1920x1080 --samples 64

# check a few frames quickly
blender -b -P build_charlie.py -- --stills 120,420,700,1300 --render stills/

# soundtrack + encode
python make_soundtrack.py soundtrack.wav
ffmpeg -framerate 24 -i frames/frame_%04d.png -i soundtrack.wav \
       -c:v libx264 -pix_fmt yuv420p -crf 18 -c:a aac -shortest charlie.mp4
```

`./render.sh` runs all of that in one go.

## How it's put together

- **Charlie** is built from soft primitives in proportions that read as Pixar (big head,
  big eyes, small body): a mustard hoodie, round glasses and swoopy hair. Eyes rotate
  to look around. Hemisphere eyelids handle blinks, sleepy looks and wide-eyed moments.
  Brows raise and furrow, and the mouth swaps between shapes (flat, smile, frown, "o",
  grin). Two-joint arms and legs are posed with a small keyframe pose system
  (`Charlie.pose`, `blink`, `talk`, `walk`).
- **Sets** are separate rooms laid out along the X axis. Charlie jumps between them on
  hard cuts, and timeline markers bind a camera to each shot.
- **Look**: Cycles with soft area lights, subsurface skin, coat and sheen materials,
  depth of field that follows Charlie's face, and AgX "Punchy" colour.
  Time of day is driven by keyframed light colour and energy plus a keyframed sky
  gradient.
- **Timeline**: `SCENES` at the top of both scripts controls each scene's length.
  Change it in both places to re-time the film.
